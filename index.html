Our Story

Two Brothers.
One Standard.

Concours Auto Detailing was founded by two brothers who grew up around cars. One became a mechanic. One became obsessed with the finish. Between them, they cover everything a car needs — and nothing gets missed.

With over ten years in the luxury automotive space, we've worked on vehicles that most people only see in magazines. We know what a Concours-standard finish looks like up close — because we've been there, studying the cars that take the trophies.

We treat every car the way Concours judges do — with a critical eye, genuine reverence, and the understanding that the details are everything.

We come to you — your driveway, your garage, your parking spot. Or drop your car at our location in Walden SW if that suits your schedule better. Either way, your car is in the same hands, held to the same standard.
